# Team transition news — article inventory

Working plan for team-level news at constructor **rebrands**, **arrivals**, and **departures**. Blends a
real-world narrative (the *why*) with game-world facts (the *record*). Source of truth for the Sonnet
copy pass. (Lineage timelines generated by `scripts/team-transitions.ts`.)

## How these fire
- **Real-world mode** progresses through the historical grids (start any year 1996-2026). At each
  season end, the historical transition for the next year is detected (`src/lib/history/transitions.ts`)
  and offered. When applied, the article fires (off-season round = `calendar.length + 1`).
- **Fictional mode** (2026 forward) only produces god-mode grid changes — those keep the existing
  **generic** entry/exit copy. The bespoke real-world copy below is keyed by `(lineageId, year,
  fromName→toName)` and only used when a transition matches a known historical one; otherwise fall back
  to generic.

## The one hard rule (do not break)
The real-world layer supplies ONLY the **unmodelled why**: ownership changes, buyouts, works entries,
title sponsors, financial collapse, the Lotus naming dispute. These can't contradict the sim because the
sim doesn't model them.

The **competitive record** (wins, poles, podiums, points, constructors' finishes, titles, which drivers
raced, who's left seatless) MUST be computed from the game world (`teamCareers[id]`, standings,
driver-market output). NEVER import real results (e.g. "Toyota's winless run", "Brawn's double title")
as fact — in this save the record may differ. State the game-world number, or say nothing.

## Standard slot sets (facts the producer computes)
- **Stint card** (rebrand retrospective + departure): aggregated over the lineage `id` for the relevant
  span — `stint_from`, `stint_to`, `stint_seasons`, `name_era` ("raced as Lotus, then Caterham"),
  `wins`, `podiums`, `poles`, `points`, `best_constructors_finish`, `constructors_titles`,
  `best_result` (biggest single game-world result), `top_driver` (most successful game-world driver for
  the lineage).
- **Departure-only**: `final_drivers` (last season's pair), `seatless` (those not re-signed in the
  off-season market = `DroppedDriver[]`).
- **Arrival-only**: `entry_year`, `grid_count`, `signed_drivers` (who the new team took from the market),
  `funding_tier`.
- **Rebrand-only**: `from_name`, `to_name`, `next_year`, `prior_record` (stint card up to the rebrand),
  `kept_drivers` / `new_drivers`.

**44 events total: 26 rebrands, 9 arrivals, 9 departures. By tier: 12 Marquee, 24 Standard, 8 Minor.**

Tiers decide only (i) article length and (ii) whether Sonnet writes bespoke copy or it uses a short
generic template:
- **Marquee** (12) — full feature: whole story arc, a quote, a game-world retrospective (stint stats +
  seatless drivers), pinned high. e.g. Toyota's exit, the Brawn buyout, Stroll's Aston Martin.
- **Standard** (24) — normal article: real-world reason + game-world facts in a few paragraphs. e.g. BMW
  entering, Renault's works return, Spyker → Force India.
- **Minor** (8) — short blurb, a sentence or two. e.g. Footwork → Arrows, Forti folding, AlphaTauri →
  Racing Bulls.

**Decisions:** (A) one bespoke article per event, all 44 (length just follows the story, no generic-only
tier). (B) each saga step is its own article. (C) one article per event, no hit+feature split.
(D) add a shared "grid grows" article for the 2010 three-team intake on top of the individual pieces.
Net: **45 articles** (44 events + the 2010 grid-grows piece). Tier = length/priority hint only.
